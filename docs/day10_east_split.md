This is your second **Split Day**, allowing the group to choose their own adventure level.

=== "🧗‍♂️ Group A: The Thrill-Seekers (Via Ferrata degli Alpini)"

    **Expectation & Rationale:** 
    Also known as the Col dei Bos Via Ferrata, this is a spectacular half-day adventure near the Falzarego Pass. It's steeper than Gran Cir but very well protected, and you'll literally be climbing alongside old WWI tunnels and lookouts. 

    * **Distance**: 4.5 km round trip
    * **Elevation Gain**: ~500m
    * **Difficulty**: ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Intermediate (Steep and exposed, requires physical exertion and pulling yourself up on the cables in places).
    * **Duration**: 4.5 hrs total (Approx. 45 min approach hike from the Strobel parking lot, 2 hrs on the secured cable route climbing through WWI positions, 30 min at the summit enjoying the panorama, and 1.5 hrs descending the hiking trail back to the car).
    * **Required Gear**: Helmet, harness, and via ferrata lanyard. Rent in Cortina the evening before from **Cortina Sport** or **Franz Sports** — typical rental ~€25-30/set.
    * **Trailhead**: Drive to the **Strobel parking lot** near Passo Falzarego (~25 min from Cortina). Free parking.

=== "☕ Group B: The Relaxers (Cortina / Lakes)"

    **Expectation & Rationale:** 
    Cortina d'Ampezzo is known as the "Pearl of the Dolomites". It's a chic, upscale town that hosted the 1956 Winter Olympics and the 2026 Winter Olympics. 

    **Core plan (regardless of lake choice):**
    * **Morning** (~2 hrs): Sleep in and stroll down **Corso Italia**, the main pedestrian shopping street lined with high-end boutiques, cafés, and gelaterias. Grab a late breakfast at one of the outdoor terraces.
    * **Midday Lunch** (~1.5 hrs): Treat yourself to a luxurious sit-down lunch at one of Cortina's upscale restaurants — you've been eating trail food for days.

    **Lake options — decision not made yet:**

    **Option A — Cortina only / Lago di Misurina (afternoon)** ✅ *no car conflict*
    * Stay in town and explore the Olympic venues (1956 & 2026), boutiques, and gelaterias.
    * **Lago di Misurina** (~2-3 hrs): Drive ~35 min to the lake (1,756m) for a flat 2–3 km partial lakeside loop. **No reservation needed and the road is open all day** — slot it in the afternoon once Group A returns with the car.

    **Option B — Lago di Braies (morning only)** ⚠️ *requires the shuttle car plan + early start*
    * **Road access critical**: The Braies road is **closed to private vehicles 9:30 AM–4:00 PM** — you must be parked **before 9:30 AM** with a **pre-booked parking slot (P4, ~€10/vehicle) at [pragsparking.com](https://www.pragsparking.com)**.
    * Leave Cortina with the whole group **~7:15 AM** under the shuttle plan (see car-sharing section below) — Group A gets dropped at the Strobel lot en route.
    * ~2 hrs: the flat 2.5 km loop around the emerald lake (rowboat jetty, chapel on the north shore), then a lakeside lunch at **Hotel Pragser Wildsee** — back in Cortina ~12:30 PM.
    * *Decision deadline*: confirm **~Sept 19** so the Braies parking slot can be reserved and the shuttle schedule is locked in.

### 🚗 Car-Sharing Plan (one rental car, 5 people)

The group only has the **single rental car**, so how the day splits depends on which plan Group B picks:

| Group B choice | Morning | Afternoon | Car handoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Cortina only** | Group A drives to the **Strobel lot** (free parking, ~25 min); Group B walks Corso Italia | Group B town/lunch; Group A returns ~1:30 PM | Group A has the car ~8:00 AM – 1:30 PM |
| **Misurina (Option A)** | Same as above | Group B takes the car (~2:30 PM) to **Misurina** (35 min, no reservation, open all day); back ~5:30 PM | Car passes A → B around 1:30–2:00 PM |
| **Braies (Option B)** | **Shuttle**: ~7:15 AM everyone drives to Strobel, drops Group A (~7:40 — coffee at the Passo Falzarego huts while B drives off); Group B keeps the car → **Braies by ~9:00 AM** | Group B lakeside lunch, collects Group A at the Strobel lot **~1:30 PM**, everyone back in Cortina ~2:00 PM | Group B owns the car all morning; Group A never drives |

*   **If Group B picks Misurina (or Cortina only):** zero conflict — Group A has the car all morning, Group B gets it after lunch.
*   **If Group B picks Braies:** it's a strict morning activity (road closes 9:30 AM), so it owns the car first; Group A just starts its ferrata ~1 hr earlier than usual via the shuttle.
*   Whatever the choice, the car is back in Cortina by early afternoon — dinner reconvene is unaffected.
